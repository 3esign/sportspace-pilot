# SportSpace E01 notice

Research team: doc. dr. Semir Poturak; prof. dr. Amra Tuzović. University of Travnik, Faculty of Technical Sciences.

This is an experimental research interface built from a technical working window, synthetic origin points, an OpenStreetMap-derived walking baseline, and a five-candidate public-source verification sample.

Two candidates have an independently documented operating or access regime. No candidate has been checked on site. The interface does not establish current public access, physical condition, population demand, health effects, or an official planning priority.

Source registry snapshot: 2026-09-19. Method run: `method_run_002`.

Interface v0.21 adds 2D/3D views and geometric reveal animation without changing method_run_002. Animation time is not travel time. Heights are mapped/estimated display context, not field measurements. Paths follow the unchanged baseline; dashed snap connections are not verified pedestrian entrances.

Version0.23 adds environmental2021 context, water fractions, three established accessibility model families and a Pareto comparison. All are secondary-data analyses. The3D tree geometry symbolizes sampled land-cover cells, not an inventory of measured trees. Regional air values are shared across local places, not street measurements. No field study is required for this computational scope; field/user work remains future work.

Website v0.30 reorganises the existing atlas into a map-first layout with on-demand results and a research overview. It displays saved v0.25 temporal/meteorological outputs and the unchanged v0.29 final paper/supplement. Historical concentration–time integrals are not inhaled dose or recommendations for today. No scientific baseline is modified.
